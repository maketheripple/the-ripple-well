About Projects Socials Get Involved Support

Indigenous Ripples

A space for Indigenous voices, stories, culture, and lived experiences. This segment explores the strength of community, the importance of listening, and the lasting impact that connection and understanding can create.

Ripple Beyond the Hard Hat

A look beyond the job site and the hard hat to the people behind the work. This segment opens conversations about mental health, pressure, resilience, and the importance of making sure workers know they are not alone.

Ripple Beyond the Roof

A closer look at homelessness and the people living beyond the walls of a home. This segment focuses on dignity, compassion, understanding, and the small acts of support that can help someone feel seen and valued.

Ripple in Recovery

Recovery is rarely a straight line. This segment shares conversations about the challenges, victories, setbacks, and personal strength found along the way, reminding us that progress can take many different forms.

Ripple Outside the Classroom

Mental health does not stop when the school bell rings. This segment explores the experiences of students, educators, and young people outside the classroom, including the pressures, relationships, and support systems that shape their lives.

Ripple Response

A space for conversations with people who respond when others are having some of their hardest moments. This segment explores mental health, resilience, stress, and the human side of those who help others in crisis.

Ripple with Heroes

A chance to hear from people who dedicate themselves to helping and protecting others. This segment looks beyond the uniform to explore the human experiences, pressures, sacrifices, and mental-health realities of our everyday heroes.

Ripple Without Limits

A celebration of people who refuse to let barriers define what they can do. This segment explores accessibility, inclusion, determination, and the many different ways people create meaningful lives and make an impact.

Ripple with Pride

A space for LGBTQ+ voices, experiences, identity, and community. This segment creates room for honest conversations about mental health, belonging, acceptance, and the strength that can come from knowing you are not alone.

Young Voices, Big Ripples

Young people have powerful stories, ideas, and perspectives that deserve to be heard. This segment gives youth a place to speak openly about mental health, their experiences, their hopes, and the changes they want to see in the world.
