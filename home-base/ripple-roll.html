Every $50 we donate gives us another roll of the dice. We roll a 20-sided die (D20), and each number on the die represents a different category of need within our community. Whatever number the die lands on determines where that $50 donation will go. One roll might help provide food. Another might support clothing, personal care items, mental health resources, shelter, or another community need.

The goal is simple:
We roll. We give. We make the Ripple.

Every $50 becomes a new opportunity to help someone who needs it — and every roll creates another ripple of kindness throughout our community.
